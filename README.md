\## 📖 Project Overview



The AI Registration Assistant is a conversational chatbot that helps students complete their internship registration through a simple chat interface.



The chatbot collects the student's:



\- Full name

\- Email address

\- Field of study

\- Programming experience



It also performs intent recognition, entity extraction, email validation, conversation management, and registration confirmation.



The project combines rule-based conversational logic with NLP and machine-learning-based intent classification.
